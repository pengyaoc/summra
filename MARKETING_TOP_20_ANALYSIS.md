## Critical Risks & Mitigation

### Risk 1: Summary Quality for Complex Texts
- **Concern:** Brothers Karamazov, The Republic are philosophically dense
- **Mitigation:** 
  - Start with chapter-level, build to themes
  - Add "Philosophy Guide" tab (like Character Guide) for key concepts
  - Invest extra time in editorial review for these 5 books

### Risk 2: Poetry Format Challenges
- **Concern:** Beowulf, Paradise Lost don't have traditional "chapters"
- **Mitigation:**
  - Use "Books" or "Cantos" or "Fyttes" as chapter equivalents
  - Showcase this as FEATURE: "Summra adapts to ANY structure"
  - Create "Epic Poetry" landing page highlighting this capability

### Risk 3: Play Format (Acts, Not Chapters)
- **Concern:** Importance of Being Earnest has 3 acts, not 20+ chapters
- **Mitigation:**
  - Summary by Act, then by Scene within each act
  - Add "Character Relationships" guide (comedy of manners = complex pairings)
  - Market as "Perfect for quick review before seeing the play"
