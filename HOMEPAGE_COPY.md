## Tone & Voice Guidelines

### Do:
✓ Be encouraging and supportive
✓ Acknowledge that classics can be intimidating
✓ Focus on understanding AND enjoyment
✓ Use "you" language (reader-centric)
✓ Be specific about features/benefits
✓ Show, don't just tell (visuals + copy)

### Don't:
✗ Sound condescending ("dumbed down")
✗ Over-promise ("become a literature expert overnight")
✗ Be too academic or stuffy
✗ Use jargon without explanation
✗ Ignore the effort reading requires
✗ Suggest Summra replaces reading

### Key Messaging Principles:
1. **Empowerment**: You CAN read classics
2. **Support**: We're here to help, not replace your reading
3. **Accessibility**: For students AND lifelong learners
4. **Quality**: AI-powered + human-curated
5. **Flexibility**: Your pace, your way
